## Direction contract
THESIS: answer first what deserves investigation, with legible evidence and manual controls.
OWN-WORLD: native macOS as fixed by the brief; SF Pro, sidebar in secondary type, thin dividers, window background, the system's configured accent color for selection, symbols and words next to states.
STORY: read the diagnosis, compare CPU/memory/I/O, select a process, examine its origin and history before acting.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 190 pt sidebar, status header, CPU and memory strip with real histories, table of the highest-consuming processes, and a source/timestamp footer.
FORM: native conventions explicitly requested by the user, with no randomized identity; the compact menu and floating panel repeat the window's language.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
